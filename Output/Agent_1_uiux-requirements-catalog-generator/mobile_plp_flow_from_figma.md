## HLD Enrichments
- Validation Scenarios:
  - [HLD] Maximum of 5 wearables only allowed. Please select less wearables.
  - [HLD] Please select less Accessories and refer the customer to a store to purchase any additional accessories.
- Negative Scenarios:
  - [HLD] When a device is selected first, then status will be changed to Selected, but not added to the cart.
  - [HLD] When a rate plan is selected first, then status will be changed to Selected, but not added to the cart.
- Boundary Scenarios:
  - [HLD] System will apply 10% tax, truncate to 2 decimals, and display in each rate
- Business Rules:
  - [HLD] Add to cart/Update cart. When Sales Calculator home page is loaded / launched, customer information will be shown instantly.
  - [HLD] An upgraded service will be shown as follows in the cart.
- Restrictions:
  - [HLD] Recommendation button will be disabled.
- Limits:
  - [HLD] Maximum of 5 wearables only allowed. Please select less wearables.
  - [HLD] System will show the list of SIM only plans where one or more rate plans can be added.
- Eligibility Rules:
  - [HLD] System allows SIM accessories for physical SIM orders.
- Compatibility Rules:
  - [HLD] Compatibility check will happen on Update cart button click.
- RTSA Rules:
  - [HLD] Possible values of StockAvailability are Available, Non-Orderable, Low Stock and Back Order.
  - [HLD] The BS: VF TBUI MSO Utilities Method:mInvokePostConfiguration() invokes BS: VF TBUI MSO Utilities Method:mPropositionNext() once for each order to maintain and check stock indic...
- Stock Rules:
  - [HLD] IMEI / serial # will be enabled when its stock indicator is In Stock.
  - [HLD] Possible values of StockAvailability are Available, Non-Orderable, Low Stock and Back Order.
  - [HLD] This section will show the repayment term of device and its recommended retail price (RRP).
- Warning Messages:
  - [HLD] Compatibility issues are shown in a popup on Update cart.
- Success Messages:
  - [HLD] Add to cart/Update cart. When Sales Calculator home page is loaded / launched, customer information will be shown instantly.
